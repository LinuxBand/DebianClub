**Debian 10**, codename **Buster**, was released on **July 6, 2019**. Its **LTS support ended on June 30, 2024**, and skipping releases is not supported — systems still on 10 should migrate step-wise via **10 → 11 → 12 → 13**, or evaluate third-party **ELTS** (until 2029-06-30).

<Callout type="error" title="Double EOL: migrate soon">

Debian 10 has had no official security updates for years, and its kernel and toolchain are far behind the current stable. Unless regulation forces you to keep it, prefer a fresh [Debian 13](/en/debian-13) install or the step-wise upgrade route.

</Callout>

## 🔄 Migration route

Debian does not support skipping releases — from 10 you must go step by step:

1. **10 → 11 (Bullseye)**: fully update, switch sources from `buster` to `bullseye`, `apt full-upgrade`, reboot and verify
2. **11 → 12 (Bookworm)**: repeat the process, updating third-party repository addresses
3. **12 → 13 (Trixie)**: the final switch — see the [upgrade guide](/en/basics/upgrade)

Back up `/etc` and your data at every step and confirm services before moving on. For old hardware with rebuildable data, **a fresh Debian 13 install is usually faster and safer than three upgrades**.
